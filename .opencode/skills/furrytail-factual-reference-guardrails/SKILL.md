Apply the Furrytail factual reference as the source of truth for all factual website content.

## Core rule
Use only facts explicitly confirmed in the Furrytail reference or explicitly provided later by the user. When a needed fact is missing, choose honest, general language instead of inventing specifics.

## Brand and company stage
- Describe Furrytail as a premium natural pet care brand for dogs and cats, launching in India.
- Treat the brand as pre-launch or newly launching.
- Do not mention sales history, customer counts, years in business, "since" dates, traction, or an existing customer base.
- Refer to the founder as a single individual, Bhargav Das, unless the user later says otherwise.
- Do not imply a large team, research department, multiple founders, or expert staff.

## Claims, certifications, and endorsements
- Do not add or preserve certification badges, seals, or claims unless the user later provides them explicitly.
- Do not state or imply medical, therapeutic, disease-prevention, or cure claims.
- Prefer safe phrasing such as "gentle and safe for daily use," "formulated without harsh chemicals," or "supports healthy skin and coat" when needed.
- Do not mention vet partnerships, vet approval, paid endorsements, expert endorsements, or named collaborators unless explicitly confirmed.

## Pricing
- Do not show a strikethrough original price unless the user has explicitly provided both the current price and the original price as real values.
- Founding or launch pricing language is acceptable only when the pricing itself is real.
- If pricing proof is uncertain, remove the unsupported comparison rather than implying a discount.

## Shipping, returns, payment, contact, and presence
- Do not invent shipping timelines, courier names, fees, or delivery windows.
- When shipping details are needed but not confirmed, use honest placeholder language such as "Shipping details will be provided at checkout" or "We're finalizing delivery timelines and will update this shortly."
- Do not invent return windows, refund day counts, or formal legal policy terms.
- Use general honest language for unresolved policy pages.
- Do not invent a physical address, showroom, social handle, follower count, or payment provider.
- Use the contact form as the primary confirmed contact method unless the user later provides a specific email or phone number.

## Existing-content cleanup rule
When editing or reviewing Furrytail pages, remove or rewrite any existing content that conflicts with these rules. This includes unsupported proof, fake discounts, fabricated policy details, invented credentials, invented timelines, invented growth claims, or implied endorsements.

## Tone for unknown facts
When a detail is unknown, write the safest true version in calm premium language. Prefer broad, honest wording over precise false wording.

## Scope discipline
Apply these rules to homepage copy, shop copy, about copy, policy pages, product-adjacent messaging, CTAs, trust language, metadata, and any future generated content for Furrytail.

Do not add extra brand facts, numbers, policy details, or claims unless the user explicitly supplies them.
