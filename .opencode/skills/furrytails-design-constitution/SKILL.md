Apply the FurryTails design constitution to every design and styling decision on this website.

## Brand character
Create a premium editorial e-commerce experience inspired by the calm, boutique restraint of brands such as Aesop, Diptyque, and Biche, while remaining unique to FurryTails.

The site should feel:
- calm
- boutique
- editorial
- sophisticated
- organic
- timeless
- premium
- quiet luxury

It must not feel:
- corporate
- like a tech startup
- like SaaS
- futuristic
- gaming-like
- colorful
- loud

## Prompting principle
Treat requests as principle-led design direction, not literal Figma-style layout instructions. When shaping or refining a section, translate the user's goal into calm luxury through restrained typography, generous whitespace, editorial photography, and clear calls to action rather than copying named brand layouts.

## Color system
Use these colors as the baseline palette:
- Primary Background: Ivory `#F8F5F1`
- Primary Text: Charcoal `#3B3A38`
- Secondary: Beige `#E9E2D7`
- Travertine `#D8CFC4`
- Stone `#BEB8AF`
- Accent: Sage `#8D9A83`
- Accent: Moss `#68735F`

Avoid:
- pure black
- pure white
- bright saturated colors
- heavy gradients

## Typography
Use a maximum of two font families:
- Headings: Cormorant Garamond
- Body: Inter

Apply:
- large headlines
- generous line spacing
- clear editorial hierarchy

Avoid:
- bold everywhere
- condensed fonts
- rounded fonts
- playful fonts

## Layout rules
Design mobile first, then scale upward.

Use:
- large whitespace
- 12-column desktop grid
- 8-point spacing system
- section spacing between 120 and 180px
- content widths around 1200 to 1320px
- text widths around 560 to 680px

Cards must breathe. Nothing should feel crowded.

## Component style
Prefer mostly square geometry.
Use rounded corners only where needed.
Use hairline borders, soft shadows, and minimal elevation.
Prefer editorial image ratios, large photography, and minimal icons.

## Animation rules
Every animation must improve understanding.

Allowed motion:
- fade
- scale from 1.00 to 1.02
- small elevation
- opacity shifts
- subtle parallax
- very slow product rotation
- scroll reveal

Forbidden motion:
- bounce
- elastic movement
- over-rotation
- continuous spinning
- flash
- particles
- floating blobs
- confetti
- decorative 3D spectacle

Use 3D only when it increases perceived craftsmanship, such as for:
- product viewer
- packaging
- ingredient explorer
- bottle rotation
- bundle builder
- gift experience

Never create decorative floating objects, random shapes, sci-fi lighting, or glass-ball effects.

## Photography direction
Use natural daylight, stone, wood, ceramic, linen, warm shadows, editorial crops, and soft contrast.
Do not use isolated Amazon-style product photos in hero sections.

## UX standard
Every page should answer:
- Can I trust this?
- Why is this premium?
- Why should I buy?
- How do I buy?

Nothing should exist purely for decoration.

## Mobile rules
Design mobile first.
Keep touch targets at least 44px.
Use thumb-friendly navigation.
Minimize typing.
Support fast checkout-style flows where relevant.
Do not rely on hover alone for core understanding or interaction. Ensure touch-friendly equivalents.

## Accessibility
Maintain at least AA contrast.
Use visible focus states.
Support keyboard navigation.
Keep typography readable.
Never sacrifice usability for aesthetics.

## Existing FurryTails-specific constraints
Preserve these established site rules unless the user explicitly changes them:
- Hover interactions default to 800ms unless the user requests a different timing.
- Desktop image hovers can transition from desaturated to full natural color while preserving the established 20% zoom behavior where already used.
- Use sharp square corners and solid flat colors. Avoid glassmorphism.
- Homepage interactions may include scroll-triggered animation, hover animation, micro-interactions, and tasteful visual effects only when they align with the minimal, premium, text-led system.

## Decision filter
Before adding or changing a visual element, ask whether it increases clarity, trust, premium perception, or purchase understanding. If it does not, simplify or remove it.
