# Furrytail — What We Need From You

The store is **built and working**. It's live at furrytailjoy.com and can already
take Cash-on-Delivery orders end to end.

Everything below is information only you can provide. It's grouped by what it
unblocks, most urgent first.

---

## 1. To accept online payments (cards / UPI / netbanking)

Razorpay approval takes several days and is outside our control, so this is the
longest lead time on the list. **Please send these first.**

- [ ] **Legal business name** exactly as registered
- [ ] **Business PAN**
- [ ] **Registered business address**
- [ ] **Bank account details** for settlements — account name, number, IFSC
- [ ] **Business phone number** and a **contact email**
- [ ] Certificate of incorporation / GST certificate / Udyam certificate,
      whichever applies

Until this clears, the store sells on **Cash on Delivery only**. That works, and
COD is typically 40–60% of orders for a new Indian brand — so this isn't
blocking launch, but it caps how much you can sell.

## 2. To issue correct invoices

**First question, and it changes everything else here: are you GST-registered?**

**If yes:**
- [ ] **GSTIN**
- [ ] **HSN code for each of the 5 products** — required by law on every invoice
- [ ] **GST rate per product** (pet grooming products are usually 18%, but please
      confirm rather than let us assume)
- [ ] **Legal entity name and address** as they should appear on invoices

**If no:**
- [ ] Just confirm it — we'll issue plain receipts instead of tax invoices

We can't guess at this. An invoice labelled "Tax Invoice" without a GSTIN, or
missing HSN codes, is a compliance problem rather than a cosmetic one.

## 3. To charge shipping correctly

Right now the store charges **₹0 delivery on every order** — meaning you absorb
the full courier cost on every sale. That may be a deliberate launch offer, but
it should be a decision rather than an accident.

- [ ] **Courier partner** — Delhivery, Shiprocket, Bluedart, India Post, other?
- [ ] **Shipping charge** — flat rate? free above a cart value? zone-based?
- [ ] **Weight and packed dimensions per product** — couriers price on these
- [ ] **Delivery timeline** to show customers (e.g. "3–5 working days")
- [ ] **COD available everywhere, or only certain pincodes?**
- [ ] **COD handling fee** — charge one (₹40–50 is common, and it reduces fake
      orders) or absorb it?
