There is mud at the rest stop, dust on the highway, a damp coat after a rainy walk, and a hotel room that wants a clean pet on its bedsheets.

This guide covers what to pack, when to use each product and how to keep bath-day calm going while you are away. Browse all four on our [travel essentials](/travel-essentials) page.

## Why waterless care belongs in your travel bag

A full bath on the road is rarely practical. Hotel bathrooms are not built for a wet Labrador, and a coat that stays damp in a cold car or an air-conditioned room is uncomfortable for your pet.

Waterless care solves the in-between moments. You clean only what needs cleaning, right where you are, and you are done in minutes. It also keeps the routine familiar. The same scents and the same gentle touch help your pet feel at home, even when home is a hundred kilometres behind you.

Our [Dry Foam Shampoo](/products/dry-foam-shampoo) is the closest thing to bath day you can carry in a backpack. Shake well, hold it 15 cm from the coat and apply 4–6 sprays from neck to tail. Massage it gently through with your hands or a soft brush and allow 60–90 seconds to dry. No water, no rinse, no towelling.

It is made for dogs and cats, and it comes in a 100 ml bottle that tucks into any bag. The Hinoki & Bamboo scent is clean and woody - hinoki cypress and fresh bamboo, the scent of a Japanese bathhouse - a quiet reminder of home after a long day on the road.

**Use it when:** your pet rolls in something on a hike, the coat smells of the car after a long drive, or you want them fresh before meeting family.

## 2. Paw Cleaner: clean paws before every car ride

Paws carry the whole trip with them: highway dust, wet mud, sand from the beach and whatever is on the hotel lobby floor. Our no-rinse [Paw Cleaner](/products/paw-cleaner) lifts it away before it reaches your car seats or bedsheets.

Pump a small amount onto each paw or onto a damp cloth, massage gently across each pad and between the toes, then wipe clean with a damp cloth or towel. One pump, thirty seconds, done. The Spearmint & Sea Salt scent feels crisp and fresh. It comes in a 150 ml bottle and is made for dogs.

**Use it when:** your dog gets back in the car after a pit stop, comes in from a walk, or is about to settle on the bed.

## 3. Defense: protection for new trails

A new place means new grass, new trails and new parks. [Defense Anti-Tick & Flea Spray](/products/anti-tick-flea-spray) is our pre-walk protection. It helps deter ticks and fleas before your dog heads out, with vetiver and cypress - no permethrin, no synthetic pyrethroids, no DEET, no fipronil.

Hours in a car can leave even the cleanest dog smelling of the journey. Our [Refreshing Mist](/products/refreshing-mist) - yuzu and ambrette seed musk - freshens the coat in seconds, so your dog is ready for the next cuddle, photo or family hello.

Hold it 25 cm from the coat and apply 3–5 sprays over the back and flanks. Let it settle naturally - don't rub it in; it dries into the coat within seconds. Avoid the eyes and mouth. It is made for dogs, in a 100 ml bottle.

**Use it when:** you arrive somewhere new, wake up in the hotel, or want a quick refresh before dinner.

## A simple travel-day routine

Use all four together and a travel day takes care of itself:

1. **Before you head out:** spray Defense over your dog's coat before any walk in a new place, and let it dry.
2. **At every pit stop:** clean paws with the Paw Cleaner before they hop back in the car.
3. **On arrival:** a few sprays of Refreshing Mist, left to settle.
4. **When it gets messy:** a Dry Foam Shampoo cleanse in place of a full bath.

Pack alongside them: a microfibre towel, a soft brush, wet wipes for the face, a water bowl and your pet's usual bedding. Familiar smells help pets settle faster in a new room. Travelling over Diwali? Read [Diwali and your pet](/journal/diwali-pet-safety-tips) too.

## FAQs

**What should I pack when travelling with my dog?**
Your dog's usual food and water bowl, bedding, lead and ID tag, plus a small grooming kit: a waterless dry shampoo, a no-rinse paw cleaner, a tick and flea repellent for new trails, a coat mist, a microfibre towel and a soft brush.

**How do I keep my dog clean while travelling?**
Clean only what needs it, where you are: a no-rinse paw cleaner at every pit stop, a waterless dry foam shampoo when the coat gets messy, and a light coat mist on arrival. Save full baths for home.

**Can I use dry shampoo on my dog while travelling?**
Yes. Furrytail's Dry Foam Shampoo is a no-rinse, leave-on safe foam for dogs and cats in a 100 ml bottle: apply 4–6 sprays, massage through and let it dry in 60–90 seconds.

**How do I protect my dog from ticks on holiday?**
Check the coat after walks in grass and on trails, especially around the ears, neck and between the toes, and use a tick and flea repellent before walks in new places. Defense is applied before the walk and left to dry.

**Which travel products are safe for cats?**
The Dry Foam Shampoo is made for dogs and cats. The Paw Cleaner, Defense spray and Refreshing Mist are for dogs only.

**How do I keep my dog's paws clean on a road trip?**
Clean them at every pit stop before your dog gets back in the car: pump a little no-rinse paw cleaner onto each paw or a damp cloth, massage between the pads and wipe clean.

**How do I stop my dog smelling of the car after a long drive?**
A light coat mist freshens a clean coat on arrival. If the coat itself is oily or stale, use a waterless dry foam shampoo instead of a fragrance.
