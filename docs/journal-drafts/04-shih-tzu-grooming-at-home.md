Few dogs are as at home in Indian apartments as the Shih Tzu. Small, affectionate and built for companionship, they're also one of the higher-maintenance coats you can share a sofa with.

The good news: most Shih Tzu grooming is small and regular rather than big and occasional. Ten minutes a day prevents most of the problems that send owners to a groomer in a hurry.

## Understanding the Shih Tzu coat

The Shih Tzu has a **long double coat** - a soft undercoat beneath longer outer hair. It doesn't shed in clumps the way some breeds do; instead, loose hair gets caught in the coat. Left alone, that's how **mats** form - especially behind the ears, under the legs, on the belly and wherever a collar or harness rubs.

Mats aren't just untidy. They hold moisture and dirt against the skin, they hurt when pulled, and they can't be properly washed or dried. That's why brushing is the foundation of everything else.

## Daily: brush, and check

- **Brush daily** with a pin brush, then follow with a metal comb to find the tangles the brush missed.
- **Work in sections**, brushing down to the skin, not just the surface.
- **Mist lightly first** - brushing a completely dry long coat can cause breakage.
- **Don't wet a matted coat.** Gently tease small tangles apart with your fingers or the comb; leave large or tight mats to a professional groomer.

Many owners in Indian cities choose a shorter **"puppy cut"**, which makes daily care far easier in heat and humidity. It's a practical choice, not a lesser one.

## The face and eyes

Shih Tzus have prominent eyes and a short muzzle, so the face needs gentle daily attention:

- **Wipe around the eyes** with a soft, damp cloth, wiping away from the eye.
- **Keep facial hair away from the eyes** - a top-knot or a neat trim helps.
- **Keep shampoo out of the eyes** at bath time; clean the face with a damp cloth instead.
- If you notice **redness, discharge, squinting or cloudiness**, see your vet rather than treating it yourself.

## Ears

Shih Tzu ears are hairy and hang down, which keeps them warm and less ventilated. Check them weekly, wipe the visible outer ear with a soft cloth, and dry it after every bath. Don't put cotton buds into the ear canal. A persistent smell, scratching or head-shaking is a reason to see your vet.

## Paws

The hair between a Shih Tzu's paw pads grows long and collects dust, mud and debris - and, in monsoon, stays damp.

- Keep the **hair between the pads trimmed** short (a groomer can do this).
- **Clean the paws after every walk** with a no-rinse [Paw Cleaner](/products/paw-cleaner) at the door.
- **Dry between the toes** afterwards.

## Bathing a Shih Tzu

Follow [the full bath ritual](/journal/how-to-bathe-a-dog-at-home), with a few Shih Tzu specifics:

1. **Brush out every tangle first.** Water turns a small tangle into a mat.
2. **Wet right down to the skin** - the double coat repels water, so take your time.
3. **Lather gently** in the direction of hair growth; don't scrub the coat into knots.
4. **Rinse thoroughly.** A long, dense coat holds shampoo easily, and residue is a common cause of itching and odour.
5. **Dry completely** - this matters more for a Shih Tzu than almost any other breed. Press with towels, then use a cool or low pet dryer while brushing, until the coat is dry to the skin.

As for how often: when the coat genuinely needs it, [not on a fixed calendar](/journal/how-often-should-you-bathe-your-dog). Between baths, a [dry bath](/journal/dry-bath-vs-wet-bath-for-dogs) keeps the coat fresh without soaking it.

## What is the best shampoo for a Shih Tzu?

Because the coat is long and the skin is often sensitive, look for:

- a shampoo **made for dogs**, never [human shampoo](/journal/can-i-use-human-shampoo-on-my-dog);
- **mild, sulphate-free cleansers** that don't strip the coat's natural oils;
- **pH-balanced** for pets;
- **built-in conditioning** to help a long coat stay soft and manageable;

## Keeping a Shih Tzu smelling fresh

- **Dry thoroughly** after every bath - a damp long coat is the most common cause of a musty smell ([why your dog still smells after a bath](/journal/why-your-dog-still-smells-after-a-bath)).
- **Freshen between baths** with the waterless [Dry Foam Shampoo](/products/dry-foam-shampoo).

## A simple Shih Tzu grooming routine

| How often | What |
|---|---|
| Daily | Brush and comb to the skin; wipe around the eyes |
| After every walk | Clean and dry the paws |
| Weekly | Check and wipe the outer ears; check for mats under legs and behind ears |
| Between baths | Dry foam for a stale or oily coat |
| When the coat needs it | Full bath, rinse thoroughly, dry completely |
| Every 4–8 weeks | Professional trim (face, paws, sanitary areas, overall length) |

## When to see a vet

Grooming keeps a coat healthy; it doesn't treat problems. See your vet for persistent itching, redness, hair loss, skin odour, eye discharge or cloudiness, ear scratching or head-shaking.

## FAQs

**How often should I groom my Shih Tzu at home?**
Brush daily, clean the paws after every walk, check the ears weekly, and bathe when the coat genuinely needs it. Most Shih Tzus also benefit from a professional trim every four to eight weeks.

**What is the best shampoo for a Shih Tzu?**
A dog shampoo that is mild, sulphate-free, pH-balanced for pets and includes conditioning ingredients for a long coat. Avoid human shampoo.

**How often should I bathe a Shih Tzu?**
There's no fixed schedule - bathe when the coat genuinely needs it, and always dry the coat completely afterwards. A dry bath can freshen the coat between washes.

**How do I stop my Shih Tzu's coat from matting?**
Brush and comb daily down to the skin, always brush out tangles before a bath, and dry the coat completely. A shorter puppy cut makes mats much less likely.

**Can I use puppy shampoo on my Shih Tzu puppy?**
Use a gentle shampoo made for pets, and check with your vet before bathing a puppy under 12 weeks.

**How do I keep my Shih Tzu smelling good?**
Dry the coat thoroughly after every bath, freshen between baths with a dry foam shampoo, wash bedding and collars regularly, and keep the ears clean and dry. A light coat mist can add fragrance to a clean coat.

**How do I clean my Shih Tzu's eyes?**
Wipe gently around the eyes daily with a soft, damp cloth and keep facial hair away from them. See your vet if you notice redness, discharge or cloudiness.
